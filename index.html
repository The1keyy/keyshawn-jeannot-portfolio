About

I am a Computer Science student at UMass Boston, NSBE President, and an Information Security Analyst Intern in a university Security Operations Center. Day to day, that means reviewing security findings, looking into suspicious activity, documenting incidents, and helping track remediation across campus systems.

Outside the SOC, I lead NSBE programming that supports academic and professional growth for engineering and technology students. I am especially interested in security engineering, cloud security, vulnerability management, detection and response, and—separately—the research questions around AI and LLM security as those systems become more widely used.
